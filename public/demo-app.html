Data roles

Controller: Selected bank (financial decisions). Processor: MADON (identity & consent). Sub-processors: AWS eu-west-1, Yapily (Open Banking connectivity), Onfido (KYC video verification). ChatGPT receives zero personal data — only a pass/fail status signal.

PSD2 / PSD3 & Strong Customer Authentication

MADON is a technical service provider under PSD2 Art. 3(j) exclusion. SCA is applied per RTS Art. 97 (two-factor: knowledge + possession). PSD3 transition standards are monitored and will be adopted upon entry into force.

MiFID II — Investment Protection

For investment and pension products, MADON facilitates MiFID II suitability assessment data collection. The bank remains responsible for the suitability determination. No investment advice is provided by MADON or by ChatGPT.

GDPR · Consent & Data Sovereignty

Consent is specific, informed, freely given, and unambiguous (GDPR Art. 4(11) / Art. 7). All processing occurs within the EEA. No transfers to third countries — fully Schrems II compliant. Data subject rights exercisable at privacy.bankai.io. DPO: dpo@bankai.io.

DORA — Digital Operational Resilience Act

MADON is a registered DORA-compliant ICT third-party service provider. All Art. 30 mandatory contractual clauses in place: 4-hour incident notification SLA, annual penetration testing, audit rights, and data portability on contract termination.

AMLD6 — AML & KYC

The bank is the AMLD6-obliged entity and is responsible for all KYC/AML Customer Due Diligence. MADON facilitates identity signal passing only. Video KYC is handled by Onfido (FCA-registered, GDPR-compliant sub-processor).

Document Analysis & Fraud Prevention

Uploaded documents are processed using MADON's ML-based document intelligence system (OCR + anomaly detection + fraud pattern matching). Processing occurs within the EEA. Documents are deleted immediately after analysis — only the outcome signal is retained in the audit log.

Data Retention

Identity data: 30 days post-session then deleted. Consent audit log: 7 years (anonymised after 30 days) as required by financial regulation. Document analysis result: audit log only. Raw documents: deleted immediately after processing.

Schrems II & Data Sovereignty

All personal data remains within the EEA at all times. MADON uses AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland) exclusively. No Standard Contractual Clauses are required as no third-country transfers occur. ChatGPT's conversation data is governed separately under OpenAI's privacy policy.
